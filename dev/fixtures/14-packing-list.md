# Packing list, one bag
Tags: #reference, #travel

Wearing:

- Slim-fit trousers, dark
- Merino t-shirt

In the bag:

- Travel trousers
- 4 t-shirts
- 4 pairs of socks
- 1 shirt
- 1 pullover
- Light down jacket
- Rain jacket
- Toiletries: toothbrush, small toothpaste, nail scissors, razor, two tiny containers for shaving cream and aftershave
- Compact charger, USB-C and phone cables
- Earphones
- Sunglasses
- E-reader
- Tote bag

Before leaving:

1. Check the passport's expiry date.
2. Download maps and boarding passes.
3. Water the plants.
