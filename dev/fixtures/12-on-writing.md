On writing things down

Plain paragraphs with no title marker and no tags. The first line becomes the title anyway.

Writing a note takes a minute; finding it a year later is the hard part. Search by words works when
you remember the words. Search by meaning helps when you only remember the idea. Tags help when
you knew, at the time, how you'd look for it later — which is rarer than one would hope.
